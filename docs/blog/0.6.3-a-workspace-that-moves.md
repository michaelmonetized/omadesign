# A workspace that moves with you

Omadesign 0.6.3 gives the welcome browser the controls you reach for when a few documents become a project.

Move across a motion thumbnail to scrub its keyframes. Ctrl-click selects individual documents; Shift-click selects a range. Alt-click opens a document in another tab, and Return opens the whole selection while keeping Welcome in front.

Right-click to reveal a file, move it into a project, clone it, make a reusable template, or send it to Trash. Drag documents onto a project to move them. Hold Alt to copy. Name collisions get numbered copies, and archived project fonts travel with the artwork.

Project folders have their own context menu: create a Vector, Raster, or Layout document, reveal the folder, move the project, or send it to Trash. Personal templates appear in the Template library and open as editable copies with no saved destination.

Typography has its own sidebar tab. The top-right icons give you Inspect, Typography, Palettes, and Brand. The centered mode group follows the rainbow from Vector through Raster, Layout, Welcome, Photo, Motion, and Agent. The green Omadesign mark brings you back to Welcome; the violet sparkle opens your agent.

Download [Omadesign 0.6.3](https://github.com/michaelmonetized/omadesign/releases/tag/v0.6.3), or update with the installer on the homepage.
