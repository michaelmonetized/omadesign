# Review, compose, and keep moving

Omadesign 0.6.2 connects more of the work between the canvas, your agent and your reviewers.

Cloud review has five reaction stamps, a highlighter and a paintbrush. Mark a flat export, open its thread, reply and resolve feedback. Marks stay on the version they were drawn on. The native review window shows the same stamps and strokes.

Wayland canvas text now supports input-method composition. Candidate windows follow the text caret; committed CJK text enters the document while unfinished composition stays out of saved files and history. The release was exercised with fcitx5 and Rime.

Raster images accept motion keys and presets. Complex motion scenes reuse prepared effect pixels, and document previews render in the background with a bounded disk cache. The measured motion improvement is in CPU composition time, not a claim about display FPS.

Artboards offer move, scale and keep-artwork options, cloning and exact sizes. Export can target the document, an artboard or a selection, with a live preview and remembered settings. New canvases can use clipboard image or native-artwork bounds.

In-app agents can work with local files across the five modes. Saved conversations reopen their documents; Stop cancels a pending reopen.

Download the Linux build from the [0.6.2 release](https://github.com/michaelmonetized/omadesign/releases/tag/v0.6.2), or update with the installer on the homepage.
