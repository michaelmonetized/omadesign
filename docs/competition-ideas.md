# Mode competition drafts

These are proposed briefs, not announced competitions. Dates, prizes, judges and final rules are unset. Keep all rounds unpublished until those decisions are made.

| Mode | Working title | Brief | Submission | Judging focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design | One Shape, Big Idea | Build a poster from one repeated geometric shape. Use scale, rhythm, colour and typography to give it a clear message. | One finished poster exported as PNG, JPEG or WebP. | Composition, typography, originality and communication. |
| Layout | The First Screen | Design a welcoming first screen for an app. Show how its hierarchy adapts between desktop and mobile. | One contact sheet with desktop and mobile layouts, exported as a flat image. | Hierarchy, clarity, adaptability and attention to detail. |
| Pixel | Ordinary to Impossible | Turn an everyday object into an imaginative scene with painting, compositing and texture. | One finished flat image; a short description of the transformation. | Craft, texture, visual integration and imagination. |
| Photo | Three Frames, One Story | Tell a story about a place, person or everyday ritual through a three-photo series. | One triptych contact sheet, with a brief caption. | Storytelling, editing, colour and consistency across the series. |
| Motion | Small Moves | Give a logo or interface element personality in a seamless 3–8 second loop. | Animated loop plus a still cover. | Timing, expression, legibility and the quality of the loop. |

Design, Layout, Pixel and Photo can use the current flat-image showcase entry pipeline. Motion must remain unpublished until animated upload, playback and review are implemented and verified. Each mode should have its own competition record, gallery and result rather than competing against unrelated formats.

A shared theme can connect a later season, but the opening briefs should stand alone so a newcomer can enter with one finished piece. Competition entry remains an explicit action on an owned, public showcase work.
