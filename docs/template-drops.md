# A year of useful starting points

52 original templates, available locally from day one. “Week” is a suggested editorial sequence, not an automatic release schedule. Every design reflows into all built-in document sizes and custom formats, with editable text and vector artwork. Dates, venue details and sample business copy are creative placeholders to change before publishing.

Use each weekly feature to show a practical edit: replace the headline, choose the right size, change two colours, then export. Invite people to share their variation. No posts or reminders are scheduled by this document.
